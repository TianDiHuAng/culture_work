亨利·詹姆斯

Henry James（亨利·詹姆斯，1843-04-15—1916-02-28）

1843 — 1916 · 美国 · 现当代 Henry James (American writer) — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

亨利·詹姆斯，纽约人，美国小说家与批评家——1915年入英国籍（Britannica）；20部长篇、112个短篇、12部剧作（B297）；'a key transitional figure between literary realism and Modernism'（现实主义与现代主义之间的关键过渡人物——B297）。'James ended this first phase of his career by producing his masterpiece, The Portrait of a Lady (1881)'（B297）；'As a picture of Americans moving in the expatriate society of England and of Italy, this novel has no equal'（B297）。'Today he is best remembered as the author of the novel The Portrait of a Lady (1881) and the novella The Turn of the Screw (1898)'（B297）。
