诺曼·梅勒

Norman Mailer（诺曼·梅勒，1923-01-31—2007-11-10）

1923 — 2007 · 美国 · 现当代 Norman Mailer — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

梅勒，新泽西Long Branch生，美国作家——'New Journalism'——'combines the imaginative subjectivity of literature with the more objective qualities of journalism'（新新闻主义：文学的想象主观性+新闻的客观性——B409）。The Naked and the Dead（裸者与死者, 1948）——索邦求学期间写——'hailed immediately as one of the finest American novels to come out of World War II'（B409）——'follows a platoon of 13 American soldiers stationed on the Japanese-held island of Anopopei in the Pacific'（B409）。The Executioner's Song (1979)——'Pulitzer Prize-winning novel based on the life of convicted murderer Gary Gilmore'（B409）。The Armies of the Night (1968)——普利策+全国图书奖（B409）。
