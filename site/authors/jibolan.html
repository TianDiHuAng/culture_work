纪伯伦

Kahlil Gibran（纪伯伦·哈利勒·纪伯伦，1883-01-06—1931-04-10）

1883 — 1931 · 黎巴嫩 · 近代 Khalil Gibran — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

纪伯伦，黎巴嫩布舍里人、移民波士顿的阿拉伯裔美国作家与画家（Britannica），以阿拉伯语与英语双语写作——'produced romantic, mystical essays, stories, poetry, and paintings on themes like love, death, nature, and longing for his homeland'（B264）。代表作《先知》（1923；'a book of 26 poetic essays'——B265）；'translated into more than a dozen languages'、'a best-selling book of popular mysticism……reached cult status among American youth for generations'（B265）。'Gibran's literary and artistic output is highly romantic in outlook and was influenced by the Bible, Friedrich Nietzsche, and William Blake'（B264）。

生平年谱
