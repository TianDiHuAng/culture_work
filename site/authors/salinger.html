J.D.塞林格

J.D. Salinger（杰罗姆·大卫·塞林格，1919-01-01—2010-01-27）

1919 — 2010 · 美国 · 现当代 Graham Greene — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

塞林格，曼哈顿生，美国作家——1951年《麦田里的守望者》'brought him lasting fame'（B316）。'relates in authentic teenage idiom his flight from the phony adult world, his search for innocence and truth'（B316）；'The humor and colorful language of The Catcher in the Rye place it in the tradition of Mark Twain's'（B316）。1953年移居新罕布什尔州科尼什——'After the success of The Catcher in the Rye, Salinger became a recluse'（B316）。'His last published work was a novella titled Hapworth 16, 1924, which appeared in The New Yorker in 1965'（B316）。
