李清照

Li Qingzhao（李清照，号易安居士，1084—约1155后）

1084 — None · 中国 · 古代 Li Qingzhao — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

李清照，济南人，'China's greatest woman poet'（中国最伟大的女诗人——Britannica），词人。'Came from a literary family and was writing praised poetry as a teenager'（文学世家、青少年即被赞——Britannica）；1101年嫁赵明诚（Britannica：Married antiquarian Zhao Mingcheng in 1101）；1129年夫亡南渡（Britannica：he died in 1129 during their flight from the Juchen takeover of Kaifeng）；1132年抵杭州、后逃金华（Britannica：Reached Hangzhou by 1132, then fled to Jinhua, Zhejiang, where she died）。'known for striking word choices and drawing on personal experience, giving her poems notable emotional intensity'（字句惊人、个人经验入诗、情感强度著称——Britannica）；'Wrote mainly ci poetry (a song form); her command of its metrical rules led her to produce one of the earliest scholarly studies of the form'（词学——Britannica）；'Produced seven volumes of essays and six of poetry, though most are lost'（七卷散文六卷诗多佚——Britannica）。'Earlier pieces show carefree vitality, while post-exile work carries a sombre, grieving quality'（前期活泼后期凄怆——Britannica）。
