王维

Wang Wei（王维，字摩诘，701—761）

701 — 761 · 中国 · 古代 Wang Wei supplementary — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

王维，祁县人（Britannica：born in Qi county, Shanxi），唐代诗人、画家与音乐家（Britannica：a famed Tang dynasty poet, painter, and musician）。721年进士（Britannica：earned the jinshi degree in 721）；安史之乱被俘（756——Britannica：later captured during An Lushan's rebellion in 756）；因《凝碧池》诗与弟王缙获救（Britannica：spared punishment after the rebellion thanks to a poem showing his loyalty and his brother's intervention）。晚年辋川别墅隐居研究佛教（Britannica：Late in life, after losing his wife and mother, he withdrew to a villa on the Wang River to study Buddhism, where the landscape inspired many of his finest poems）。尤以水墨单色山水著称（Britannica：especially known for ink monochrome landscapes, particularly snowscapes, using the 'breaking the ink' (pomo) technique）；17世纪董其昌尊为南宗画祖（Britannica：In the 17th century, Dong Qichang named him founder of the Southern school of painter-poets）；'ranked alongside Li Bai and Du Fu among the Tang dynasty's greatest poets'（与李白杜甫并列唐代最伟大诗人——Britannica）。761年卒（Britannica）。

生平年谱

701 — 生于祁县（Britannica：born in Qi county, Shanxi）。 Wang Wei supplementary — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）
721 — 进士及第（Britannica：earned the jinshi degree in 721）。 Wang Wei supplementary — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）
756 — 安史之乱被俘（Britannica：captured during An Lushan's rebellion in 756）——因诗获救（Britannica）。 Wang Wei supplementary — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）
761 — 卒（Britannica：died 761），年六十一。 Wang Wei supplementary — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

代表作品

  • 王维诗选：通行选集（本库立目）
  • 辋川集（与裴迪唱和）：山水诗（通说）
  • 鸟鸣涧、鹿柴、送别：名篇（通说）

风格特点

  • '诗中有画，画中有诗'（苏轼评——通说待核）。
  • '空'与'静'的禅意：Britannica——'the landscape inspired many of his finest poems'（辋川佛教与山水——Britannica）。
  • 水墨'破墨'山水画（Britannica：ink monochrome landscapes, particularly snowscapes, using the pomo technique）。
