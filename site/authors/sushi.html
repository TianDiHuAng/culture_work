苏轼

Su Shi / Su Dongpo（苏轼/苏东坡，1037-01-08 — 1101-08-24）

1037 — 1101 · 中国 · 古代 Su Shi (Su Dongpo) — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

苏轼，字子瞻，号东坡居士，眉山人，宋代诗人、散文家、书法家与画家（Britannica：a Song Dynasty poet, essayist, calligrapher, and official from Meishan, Sichuan）。'excelled in the civil service examinations'（科举及第——Britannica）；'held many posts, though he was banished to Huangzhou in 1079 by Wang Anshi over opposition to reforms, and later to Hainan Island in 1094'（1079年贬黄州、1094年贬海南——Britannica）；'Known for his optimism, he mastered shi, ci, fu, and essays'（精通诗词赋文——Britannica），'became known as the founder of the haofang ('heroic abandon') school of writing'（豪放派开创者——Britannica）。1101年卒（Britannica：died 1101）。
