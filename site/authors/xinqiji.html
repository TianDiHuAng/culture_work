辛弃疾

Xin Qiji（辛弃疾，字幼安，号稼轩，1140—1207）

1140 — 1207 · 中国 · 古代 Xin Qiji — Encyclopaedia Britannica（百科全书）

辛弃疾，历城（今济南）人，南宋词人（Britannica：a Chinese poet of the Southern Song dynasty）。'He became a soldier hoping to avenge the Jin victory over the Song, but after moving south in 1162 he found no chance to carry out his great ambitions'（士兵出身志复金占宋土、1162年南归后'没有机会实现大志'——Britannica）；'After a military career of nearly 20 years, he retired to Shangrao, where he read and wrote poetry'（近20年军旅后退隐上饶读书写诗——Britannica）。'He wrote 623 ci (poems set to existing musical patterns), praised by many critics as the finest of the Southern Song, notable for expanding the form's emotional depth'（623首词——许多批评家赞为南宋最佳、拓展词的情感深度——Britannica）。1207年卒于上饶（Britannica：died in Shangrao, Jiangxi, aged 67）。
